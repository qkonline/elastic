Elastic

Privacy

Last changed 24 November 2026

Elastic is made and run by Qaisar Khan. It has no ads and no tracking, and your plan isn't sold or used for anything but showing it to you.

What's kept

  • On your device: your whole plan, so it works offline.
  • In your account: your email, your name, the passkeys you add (their public half only, and the device they were made on), and your plan: days, tasks, notes, habits, goals, reviews and the settings you share between devices.
  • For signing in: codes last 10 minutes. Counts of codes asked for, by email and by network address, are kept for up to two days to stop abuse.
  • When you erase or import: your plan as it was before is kept for 30 days so you can bring it back.

Who helps run it

  • Cloudflare hosts the app and stores accounts. It sees your network address, as any host does, and keeps short-lived logs.
  • Resend sends the sign-in codes and notes about your account, so it has your email address and what those emails say.
  • Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft deliver notifications if you turn them on. The pushes are empty: they learn when one is sent, not what it says.
  • Boardo, the shared boards app from the same maker, only if you link it: Elastic reads the cards assigned to you, and sends back when you work on them and finish them.

The fonts and code come from this site. Nothing is loaded from anywhere else, except Cloudflare's check that you're a person if it's turned on for the sign-in page.

Your choices

  • Take it with you: Settings, Your data, Export.
  • Erase it: Settings, Your data, Erase everything.
  • Delete the account: Settings, Account, Sync across devices, Delete account. The account and the plan it holds go at once. Each device keeps its own copy until you erase it there.

An account's sign-ins end after a year without use. For anything else, including a copy of what's held about you or a correction, write to kaiser@kaiserkhan.com.
